Standing Committee on Access to Information, Privacy and Ethics

Report on the Protection of Personal Information in the Digital Age

Fourth Report, 44th Parliament, 1st Session

The Standing Committee on Access to Information, Privacy and Ethics has the honour to present its fourth report.

Pursuant to its mandate under Standing Order 108(3)(h), the Committee has studied the collection, use, and protection of personal information by federal government institutions and private sector organizations subject to federal privacy legislation.

The Committee held twelve meetings between September and December 2025, hearing from over fifty witnesses including the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, representatives from major technology companies, civil society organizations, and academic experts in privacy law.

The Committee report makes twenty-seven recommendations organized around four themes: legislative reform of the Privacy Act, enhanced enforcement powers for the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, transparency requirements for automated decision-making systems, and measures to protect the privacy of children and vulnerable populations online.

The Committee recommends that the government introduce comprehensive privacy reform legislation within twelve months and that adequate resources be allocated to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner to fulfill its expanded mandate.
