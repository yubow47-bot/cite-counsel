Statement by the Minister of Justice and Attorney General of Canada on the Appointment of Judicial Advisors

March 3, 2026 — Ottawa, Ontario

Today, the Honourable Catherine Tremblay, Minister of Justice and Attorney General of Canada, issued the following statement regarding the appointment of members to the Judicial Advisory Committees across Canada:

"The appointment of qualified and diverse candidates to the federal judiciary is one of the most important responsibilities of the Minister of Justice. Judicial Advisory Committees play a vital role in this process by assessing candidates for judicial appointment and providing the Minister with confidential recommendations.

I am pleased to announce the appointment of new members to the Judicial Advisory Committees for Ontario, British Columbia, Quebec, and the Atlantic region. These appointments reflect the government's commitment to a judicial appointments process that is transparent, accountable, and promotes diversity on the bench.

The new members bring a wealth of experience from the legal profession, academia, and community organizations. Their diverse backgrounds and perspectives will strengthen the work of the committees in identifying candidates who demonstrate the highest standards of professional excellence and personal integrity.

The membership of each Judicial Advisory Committee includes representatives from the judiciary, the provincial or territorial law society, the Canadian Bar Association, and the law enforcement community. The committees also benefit from the participation of members appointed by the provincial or territorial government.

I would like to thank the outgoing members for their dedicated service to the judicial appointments process and for their contribution to maintaining public confidence in Canada's judiciary."
