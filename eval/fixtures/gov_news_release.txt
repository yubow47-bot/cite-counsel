Government of Canada Announces New Funding for Northern Infrastructure

OTTAWA — The Honourable Marc Leblanc, Minister of Transport, today announced an investment of $475 million over five years to improve transportation infrastructure in Canada's northern territories.

This funding, delivered through the National Trade Corridors Fund, will support the construction and rehabilitation of all-season roads, airport upgrades, and marine facilities in Yukon, Northwest Territories, and Nunavut. The investment is part of the government's broader commitment to strengthening connectivity and economic development across all regions of Canada.

"Reliable transportation links are essential to the quality of life and economic prosperity of northern communities," said Minister Leblanc. "This investment will create jobs, improve safety, and ensure that northern residents and businesses have access to the infrastructure they need to thrive."

The funding will be allocated through a competitive application process administered by Transport Canada, with project selection expected to begin in spring 2026.

Projects will be evaluated based on their potential to improve safety, enhance economic development opportunities, increase climate resilience, and strengthen connections between northern communities and southern markets.
