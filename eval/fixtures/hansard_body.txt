House of Commons Debates
Official Report (Hansard)
Volume 151, Number 56
43rd Parliament, 2nd Session

The House met at 11:00 a.m. on Monday, January 25, 2021.

GOVERNMENT ORDERS

Mr. David Mitchell (Leader of the Opposition): Mr. Speaker, Canadians are looking to this government for a clear plan on infrastructure investment and regional development. The House of Commons has debated this matter extensively during the current session. Can the Minister confirm that funding commitments for the northern transportation corridor remain on schedule?

Hon. Sarah Chen (Minister of Infrastructure): Mr. Speaker, our government has committed over twelve billion dollars to strategic infrastructure projects across every province and territory. The parliamentary debates on this subject reflect the broad consensus that investment in northern connectivity is essential to economic growth.

Ms. Rebecca Torres (Portage—Lisgar): Mr. Speaker, the parliamentary committee on transport has raised concerns about the pace of project approvals. What specific measures is the government taking to accelerate the regulatory review process?

The debate continued on infrastructure investment priorities, with members from all recognized parties contributing to the discussion. Several members referenced the need for enhanced federal-provincial coordination mechanisms.
