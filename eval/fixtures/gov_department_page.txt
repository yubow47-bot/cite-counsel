Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada

About ISED

Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) works with Canadians in all sectors of the economy and in all parts of the country to improve conditions for investment, enhance innovation performance, increase Canada's share of global trade, and build a fair, efficient, and competitive marketplace.

Our Mandate

ISED is responsible for a broad portfolio of programs and services that help small businesses grow, support research and innovation, promote tourism and foreign investment, and provide economic and market data to help inform policy decisions. The department also administers Canada's framework legislation for intellectual property, competition, bankruptcy, and corporations.

Key Priorities

The department's key priorities for the current fiscal year include expanding access to high-speed internet in rural and remote communities through the Universal Broadband Fund, supporting the growth of Canada's clean technology sector through the Strategic Innovation Fund, advancing Canada's Digital Charter to ensure that Canadians' privacy is protected in the digital economy, and working with provinces and territories to reduce interprovincial trade barriers under the Canadian Free Trade Agreement.

Ministers

The Honourable Minister of Innovation, Science and Industry is supported by the Minister of Small Business, Export Promotion and International Trade, the Minister of Rural Economic Development, and the Minister of Science and Sport. Parliamentary secretaries assist the Ministers with specific portfolio responsibilities and stakeholder engagement.

Departmental Reports

ISED publishes annual reports on its activities and performance, including the Departmental Plan, the Departmental Results Report, and reports on the administration of specific legislation. These documents are tabled in Parliament and made available to the public through the ISED website and the Library of Parliament's online catalogue.
