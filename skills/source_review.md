# Reviewing source identity and coverage
> Compare a retrieved result with the user's target and state what the source actually establishes.

Before relying on a result, compare its title, author or issuing body, date,
jurisdiction, and version with the requested work. Similar names and matching
numbers can refer to different works or legislative sessions. An empty search
result describes only the collection and query searched.

For example, a result titled *Victims Bill of Rights* does not answer a request
for the *Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms*. A news story reporting a
judgment is evidence of the story's content; it is not the judgment's text.

When evidence is incomplete, state the part established by the material read,
the unresolved identity or coverage question, and any inference separately.
